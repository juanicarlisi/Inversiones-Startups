# Pedido a la American Bible Society (en inglés)

**Para:** área de permisos y licencias de la American Bible Society (contacto a confirmar en bibles.com → Rights and Permissions;
en las búsquedas aparece licensing@americanbible.org).
**Asunto:** Ministry licensing request — Reina-Valera 1960 in a youth Bible app (Latin America)

---

Dear American Bible Society Licensing Team,

We are a small team building **Senda**, a free mobile app that helps young people in Latin America read the Bible every day and
learn it through games (quizzes, reading streaks and a "full armor of God" challenge based on Ephesians 6). The app will launch on
Android and iOS in December 2026, in Spanish.

Most evangelical churches in our region read the **Reina-Valera 1960**, and our users ask for it. We would like to include the
full RVR1960 text in the app's Bible reader. Some important facts about how we would use it:

- **Reading the Bible in Senda is free forever and never shows ads.** Ads and an optional subscription exist only in the game
  sections, never next to the biblical text.
- The text would be shown **unaltered**, with the full copyright notice and the trademark acknowledgment wherever it appears.
- We expect around 5,000–10,000 monthly active users in 2027.
- We are happy to link to your resources and to credit the Bible Societies inside the app.

We are aware of API.Bible's commercial plans. Before subscribing, we would like to ask whether the American Bible Society or the
United Bible Societies offer a **ministry or non-profit-friendly license** for an app like ours, and what the terms and pricing
would be.

Thank you for your work making Scripture available to everyone. We look forward to your answer.

Kind regards,
The Senda Team
