# Pedido a Biblica (NVI) — para pegar en su formulario

**Dónde:** formulario «Permission Request Form» de Biblica (https://www.biblica.com/permission-request-form/). Si el formulario
pide datos de empresa, se completa con el nombre «Senda» y el correo del proyecto.
**Texto para el campo de descripción del uso (en inglés):**

---

We are a small team building **Senda**, a free mobile app (Android and iOS, Spanish, launching December 2026) that helps young
people in Latin America read the Bible daily and learn it through games: quizzes, reading streaks and a "full armor of God"
challenge based on Ephesians 6.

We already include Biblica's **Open Nueva Biblia Viva** under its CC BY-SA license, with full attribution, and our users love it.
Many of them also read the **Nueva Versión Internacional (NVI)**, so we would like to request a license to display the complete NVI
text in the app's Bible reader.

- Reading the Bible in Senda is **free forever and never shows ads**. Ads and an optional subscription exist only in the game
  sections, never next to the biblical text.
- The text would be shown unaltered, with Biblica's full copyright and trademark notice.
- We expect 5,000–10,000 monthly active users in 2027.

Could you let us know whether a license is possible for this use, and the terms and pricing? We are also open to accessing the
NVI through a partner (for example, API.Bible) if that is your preferred channel.

Thank you for your ministry.
The Senda Team
appsenda.ok@gmail.com
