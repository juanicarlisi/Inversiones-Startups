Dear Tyndale Permissions Team,

We are a small team building **Senda**, a free mobile app that helps young people in Latin America read the Bible every day and
learn it through games (quizzes, reading streaks and a "full armor of God" challenge based on Ephesians 6). The app will launch on
Android and iOS in December 2026, in Spanish.

Many young people in our churches read the **Nueva Traducción Viviente (NTV)** and ask us for it. We would like to offer the full
NTV text in the app's Bible reader, under these conditions:

- **Reading the Bible in Senda is free forever and never shows ads.** Ads and an optional subscription exist only in the game
  sections, never next to the biblical text.
- The text would be shown **unaltered**, with the full copyright notice ("Usado con permiso de Tyndale House Publishers") wherever
  it appears.
- We expect around 5,000–10,000 monthly active users in 2027.
- We would be glad to link to Tyndale Español resources inside the app.

Could you tell us whether a license for this use is possible, and under what terms and pricing? If you prefer to license it through
a provider (for example, API.Bible), that would also be helpful to know.

Thank you for your work making Scripture available to everyone. We look forward to your answer.

Kind regards,
The Senda Team
