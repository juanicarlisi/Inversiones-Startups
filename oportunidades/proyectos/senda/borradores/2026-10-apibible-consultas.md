Dear API.Bible Support Team,

We are building Senda, a Spanish-language mobile app (Android and iOS) that helps young people in Latin America read and learn the
Bible. Tyndale kindly told us that the Nueva Traducción Viviente (NTV) is licensed electronically only through API.Bible, so we would
like to license it with a Pro plan. Bible reading in Senda is always free and never shows ads; the app is commercial because it has an
optional subscription and short ads after some game rounds.

Before we purchase, could you please help us with four questions?

1. **Monthly End Users.** For the NTV fee, do you count every person who opens Senda in a month, or only the users who actually access
   the NTV text through our API.Bible application? Many of our users will read other (open-access) versions.
2. **Price per tier.** What is the monthly NTV fee for each end-user range (0–1K, 1K–5K, 5K–25K, 25K–100K), and how is the fee set
   above 100K?
3. **Offline reading.** Is it compliant to store the NTV encrypted on the device for offline reading, refreshing it at least every
   30 days, with copy/export disabled and printing limited to 100 verses?
4. **Read aloud.** May users listen to the NTV with the phone's built-in text-to-speech voice (spoken on the device, nothing recorded,
   stored or sent to any AI service)?

Thank you for your help and for your work making Scripture available.
